About Me

Who I am and what I care about.

I am a Computer Science student at Howard University who pairs technical development with business problem-solving: gathering requirements, translating them into user stories, and delivering AI-assisted, data-driven solutions in deadline-driven settings. I like work I can measure, from 40% fewer client risk oversights to a 30% gain in daily operational efficiency through automated data pipelines.

It started with a summer at Apple, where I built a game that became a finalist in the DC Can Code showcase. Today I split my time between classes, building projects like the Community Resource Finder, and the NSBE and cybersecurity communities on campus. I graduate in May 2027. Outside of tech, I crochet, bake and watch anime.
